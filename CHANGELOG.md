## [1.0.0] - 2026-03-24

### Added
- **100% Free & Completely Unrestricted**: Zero subscriptions, paywalls, premium tiers, trial limits, or locked features. Every single tool, template, and export is freely available.
- **Glassmorphism Design System**: Pastel glass color tokens (`#F8F7FC`, Lavender `#DCD5FA`, Soft Pink `#F7DCE8`, Mint `#D7F0E3`, Powder Blue `#DCEBFA`, Peach `#F9E3D3`), light/dark optical contrast, and rounded surfaces (24dp).
- **Android Shell Toggle**: Realistic device container with dynamic status bar (5G, WiFi, battery, live clock), punch-hole camera, and gesture navigation bar.
- **Caption Studio**: Multi-factor post creator with live character limits for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook, Threads, and Pinterest.
- **Hybrid Content Engine**: High-entropy offline rule-and-template engine coupled with an optional server-side Gemini 3.8 Flash proxy.
- **Refinement Actions**: 1-click tools for Make Shorter, Expand, Boost Hook, Improve CTA, Add/Strip Emojis, and Tone Shifts.
- **Hashtag Toolkit**: Categorized tag discovery (Broad, Niche, Industry, Campaign), custom group organizer, multi-pack merging, and deduplication.
- **Bulk Content Generator**: Multi-line batch topic input, concurrent generation, card editing, and CSV/TXT export.
- **Content Calendar**: Interactive monthly and weekly planning views, post status management (`Idea`, `Draft`, `Planned`, `Published`, `Archived`), and campaign tagging.
- **Brand Voice Manager**: Reusable brand voice profiles storing audience, tone, signature vocabulary, prohibited words, and default CTAs.
- **Template Library**: 16+ pre-seeded copywriting formulas with interactive variable replacement modals.
- **Data Persistence**: Offline-first storage with full JSON backup/restore and CSV export with RFC-4180 escaping.
- **Full-Stack Server**: Express backend (`server.ts`) proxying Gemini 3.8 Flash calls and mounting Vite development middleware.
