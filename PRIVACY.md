# SocialMuse Studio — Privacy Policy & Data Architecture

**Effective Date**: March 2026

SocialMuse Studio is designed with a **privacy-first, local-storage architecture**.

---

## 1. Zero Tracking & No Account Requirement
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: SocialMuse Studio contains zero trackers, analytics beacons, or behavioral monitoring scripts.
- **Local Storage**: All drafts, templates, custom brand voice profiles, hashtag groups, and calendar entries are saved directly in your browser or local device storage.
- **No Account Lock-in**: You are not forced to register an account or share personal information to use the software.

---

## 2. Gemini AI Integration (Optional)
- When you explicitly invoke the Gemini AI enhancement features, the prompt parameters (topic, tone, category, and desired length) are transmitted securely to Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash model via a server-side proxy.
- Secrets are never exposed to the client browser.
- Your saved drafts, private calendars, and local profiles remain entirely on your device and are never harvested for model training.

---

## 3. Data Export & Deletion
- You retain complete ownership of your data.
- You can export a complete JSON backup at any time from **Settings > Data Backup & Portability**.
- You can wipe all local storage data via the **Reset Local Database** option in Settings.

---

## 4. No Payment Information or Financial Data
- The application contains no payment gateways, in-app billing processors, checkout forms, or payment storage.
- SocialMuse Studio will never request, store, or process your credit card, banking, or billing credentials.
